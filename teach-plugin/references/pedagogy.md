# Teach pedagogy

## Contents

- Question selection

## Question selection

Make reading active by giving the learner one specific target before they read. Favor questions that ask:

- what problem the material solves;
- why a mechanism behaves as described;
- how stages or components relate;
- how two concepts differ;
- what assumptions support an argument;
- what consequence follows from a rule;
- what a diagram, table, caption, or example demonstrates;
- what should happen in a nearby case and why.

Avoid isolated vocabulary hunts unless the term itself is central. Choose the next question only after resolving the current one.

## Answer discussion

Start from the learner's answer. When it is incomplete or incorrect, identify the missing relationship without turning the interaction into a verdict. Give a hint or explanation, then invite a revised explanation in the learner's own words.

Understanding can incorporate the teaching explanation. The durable endpoint is the learner's strongest correct model, not a catalogue of earlier mistakes.

## Page boundaries

Use rendered images as the authority for content, layout, figures, and semantic boundaries. A topic may continue across a nominal TOC boundary or share a page with the following topic. Use TOC entries to navigate, then judge the teaching unit from the page itself.

Briefly identify blank, copyright, or administrative pages and continue. Tell the learner whenever the target page changes.

## Flashcard judgment

Every card creates future review work. Create a compact deck that preserves knowledge with durable retrieval value:

- central definitions and concepts;
- causal mechanisms;
- important distinctions and system relationships;
- ordered procedures, commands, formulas, and rules;
- constraints, exceptions, and facts that cannot be reconstructed reliably;
- concepts required for later material;
- meaningful knowledge in diagrams or figures.

Give each front one clear retrieval target. Put enough on the back to recover the knowledge accurately without turning it into an essay. Keep related steps together when their sequence carries meaning. Cite the physical source pages.

## Session endings

When the learner stops, abandon any unanswered active question, save cards for material already understood, then log the precise continuation point. Do not create cards from an explanation the learner has not yet made sense of.
